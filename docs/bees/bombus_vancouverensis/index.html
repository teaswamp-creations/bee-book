Vancouver bumblebee

Apidae
Vancouver Island Lower Mainland Coast Galiano

Observation notes

First documented by Cresson, 1878a, with extensive historical record in the Pacific Maritime. Biodiversity Galiano Life List: 1987, Galiano. NBSBC Bee Atlas Database: 2021, 2022, 2023; 2022, 2023, Vancouver Island. iNaturalist: widespread throughout the Pacific Maritime, from 1978.
