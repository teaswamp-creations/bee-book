Bombus flavifrons

Yellow fronted bumblebee

Apidae
Vancouver Island Lower Mainland Coast Galiano

Observation notes

First documented by Handlirsch, 1891, with extensive historical record in the Pacific Maritime. Tommasi et al. 2004: Greater Vancouver Area. Ratti et al. 2008: Fraser Valley. Biodiversity Galiano Life List: 1970, Galiano. Guzman et al., 2023: 2005; 2007; 2009, 2010; 2012; 2014, 2015; 2017, Vancouver Island. 2011, Abbotsford; 2013; 2015; 2016, Lower Mainland. NBSBC Bee Atlas Database: 2021; 2022, Vancouver Island, Saltspring; 2022, Lower Mainland, Squamish; 2023, Greater Vancouver; Vancouver Island, 2023, Galiano Island. iNaturalist: widespread throughout the Pacific Maritime, from 2008.
